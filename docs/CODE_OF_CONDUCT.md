# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to:

- **Respect** each other's ideas and contributions.
- **Be kind** in all communication.
- **Act** in good faith to resolve disagreements.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning remarks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention.

## Enforcement

Instances of harassment or other violations of the Code of Conduct may be reported by contacting the project maintainers at `maintainers@studentshousing.example.com`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from the project, at the discretion of the maintainers.
